About this instrument

The Surzion Artery Forceps Robert are long, curved hemostatic forceps designed for grasping, clamping, and occluding deeply situated blood vessels during thoracic, abdominal, and general surgical procedures. Featuring long shaft shanks and fine transverse-serrated jaws, they provide surgeons with extended reach and reliable hemostatic control in deep surgical cavities.

Usage & Applications

Essential for general, thoracic, and vascular surgeons operating in deep or hard-to-reach anatomical fields where extended reach is required.
